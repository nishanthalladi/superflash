# Superflash colours

| role | hex |
| --- | --- |
| gold (the bolt, the outline) | `#FCDF20` |
| tan (the field) | `#E0C9A6` |
| brown (strokes, text on tan) | `#C0996B` |
| cream (alternate field) | `#F7F2EC` |

`logo.png` is the full mark: gold bolt on tan, gold rounded outline.
`bolt.png` is the flat bolt alone on transparent.
`bolt-3d.png` is the rounded one, and it is what the favicon comes from: cropped
to the bolt, padded square, scaled to 512. At 16px the tan square swallowed the
bolt, and the untrimmed transparent margin shrank it to a thin slash — trimming
first is what makes it read.

Earlier try at the field was `#C0996B` — too close to the gold in value, so the
bolt stopped popping. Keep brown for lines and type, not for fills behind gold.
